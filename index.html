FIVE TIMES A DAY · 5 MINUTES

Five-Times-a-Day Ritual

Choose one affirmation that feels right for this moment. Take each part for about one minute.

  1. Pause and Take 3 Deep Breaths 1 minute

    Close your eyes or soften your gaze. Inhale deeply through your nose, exhale through your mouth. With each exhale, silently say: “I release what does not serve me.”

  2. Centering in Length, Width, and Depth 1 minute

  3. Affirmation 1 minute

    Choose one affirmation that resonates with you for this moment. Repeat it silently or aloud, feeling its truth.

  4. Gentle Touch 1 minute

    Place your hands on your heart, face, or any part of your body that feels good. Offer yourself gentle touch—for example, rub your arms, hold your hands, or place your palm on your cheek. Imagine warmth and love flowing from your hands into your body.

  5. Mindful Breathing 1 minute

    Return to natural breathing. Focus on the sensation of your breath—cool air in, warm air out. Silently repeat: “I am held by love. I am connected to all that is.”

Suggested Affirmations

Choose one affirmation per session that aligns with your intention for the day or the time of day.

Morning 6:30–7:30 AM

  • “I greet this day with an open heart and a disciplined mind.”
  • “I am a channel for love and wisdom.”
  • “Today, I choose to love myself”

Midday 12:00–1:00 PM

  • “I am present in this moment. My actions are guided by love.”
  • “I release stress and embrace peace.”
  • “I am connected to the universal intelligence.”

Afternoon 3:00–4:00 PM

  • “I release what no longer serves me. I am filled with renewed energy.”
  • “I trust the flow of life.”
  • “I am gentle with myself and others.”

Evening 6:00–7:00 PM

  • “I reflect on my day with compassion.”
  • “I honor my journey with love.”
  • “I am grateful for the lessons and love in my life.”

Night 9:00–10:00 PM

  • “I release this day with trust. I am held by the universe’s love.”
  • “My heart is open to giving and receiving love.”
  • “I surrender to rest, knowing I am supported.”
